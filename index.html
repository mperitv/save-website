You Ship You Save

Make your own open-source project, ship it, we donate to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital to save children's lives!!

Here's how it works:

You ship a project that you made while tracking the hours with hackatime. You can do everything; a website, a game, a robot... both hardware and software are welcome

We ship a donation to St. Jude Children's Research Hospital to support open research. This will help find cures especially for kids and you will be one of the people who make it possible!!
